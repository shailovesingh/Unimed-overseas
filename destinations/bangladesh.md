## Eligibility Criteria & Admissions Checklist

Bangladesh medical admissions are highly structured and depend on GPA matching:

1. **Academic Performance:** Minimum aggregate GPA of 7.0 in 10th and 12th Standard Exams combined, with a minimum GPA of 3.5 in Biology.
2. **Gap Year Limit:** A maximum of 1-year gap between 10th and 12th, and maximum 1-year gap after 12th.
3. **NEET-UG:** Qualified scorecard mandatory for registration and subsequent Indian licensing exams.
