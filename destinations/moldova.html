Study MBBS in Moldova 🇲🇩

Moldova offers a brilliant opportunity for international students to pursue high-standard medical training in Eastern Europe. Renowned state medical universities like Nicolae Testemitanu State University of Medicine and Pharmacy provide English-taught programs with extensive clinical training that are globally recognized by major medical boards including the GMC (UK) and NMC (India).

Eligibility Criteria & Admissions Checklist

Moldova state admissions are highly selective and processed on a secure application portfolio:

  1. Academic Performance: Minimum 50% aggregate score in 12th Standard Board Exams in PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology).
  2. Age Threshold: Must have completed 17 years of age on or before December 31 of the year of admission.
  3. NEET-UG: Qualified scorecard mandatory for registration and subsequent Indian licensing exams.

⏳ Admissions Note: Admissions are extremely limited for international students. Early registration is recommended to complete secure visa verification protocols. Contact our team to book your counseling session.
