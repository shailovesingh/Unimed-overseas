Study MBBS in the Philippines 🇵🇭

The Philippines has long been a top destination for international medical students seeking high-quality, practical medical education. The country's medical curriculum is closely modeled on the American system, offering extensive clinical rotations, high English proficiency, and tropical climates, making it an excellent preparation path for global licensure exams.

Program Quick Overview

Parameter Details
Degree Awarded Doctor of Medicine (MD / equivalent to Indian MBBS)
Course Duration 5.5 to 6 Years (BS + MD format fully compliant with regulations)
Medium of Instruction 100% English (Official Language Environment)
Clinical Affiliation High-volume Public and Private General Hospitals
Recognitions WHO, NMC (India), ECFMG (USA), and global medical boards

Eligibility Criteria & Admissions Checklist

Admissions to leading Philippine medical colleges are processed directly based on profile evaluations:

  1. Academic Performance: Minimum 50% aggregate score in 12th Standard Board Exams in PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology).
  2. Age Threshold: Must have completed 17 years of age on or before December 31 of the year of admission.
  3. NEET-UG: Qualified scorecard mandatory for registration and subsequent Indian licensing exams.

⏳ Admissions Note: The dual BS-MD program is highly recognized. Student visas are processed directly through the Philippine Embassy. Contact our team today to get your profile evaluated for direct admission.
