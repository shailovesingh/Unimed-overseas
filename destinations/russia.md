## Eligibility Criteria & Admissions Checklist

Getting admitted to a premium Russian medical school is straightforward and based primarily on your secondary school scores:

1. **Academic Performance:** Minimum 50% aggregate in 12th Standard Board Exams in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (40% for reserved categories).
2. **Age Threshold:** Must have completed 17 years of age on or before December 31 of the year of admission.
3. **NEET-UG:** Qualified scorecard mandatory for Indian candidates to be eligible for the NMC licensure path.

> ⏳ **Admissions Note:** Government seats fill up rapidly on a rolling basis. Direct university admissions are processed directly without third-party commission scales. Contact Unimed to lock in your seat and start your visa paperwork.
