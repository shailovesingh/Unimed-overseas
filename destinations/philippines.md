## Eligibility Criteria & Admissions Checklist

Admissions to leading Philippine medical colleges are processed directly based on profile evaluations:

1. **Academic Performance:** Minimum 50% aggregate score in 12th Standard Board Exams in PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology).
2. **Age Threshold:** Must have completed 17 years of age on or before December 31 of the year of admission.
3. **NEET-UG:** Qualified scorecard mandatory for registration and subsequent Indian licensing exams.
