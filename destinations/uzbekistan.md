## Eligibility Criteria & Admissions Checklist

Uzbekistan state university admissions are processed directly based on profile evaluations:

1. **Academic Performance:** Minimum 50% aggregate score in 12th Standard Board Exams in PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology).
2. **Age Threshold:** Must have completed 17 years of age on or before December 31 of the year of admission.
3. **NEET-UG:** Qualified scorecard mandatory for registration and subsequent Indian licensing exams.

> ⏳ **Admissions Note:** Tashkent and Samarkand medical institutes are highly sought-after. Seats are allocated on a rolling, first-come first-served merit basis. Direct university admissions are processed with complete transparency. Contact our team to book your counseling session.
