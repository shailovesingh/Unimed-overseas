Study MBBS in Nepal 🇳🇵

Nepal has long been a highly trusted and comfortable study destination for Indian medical students. Renowned medical colleges in Kathmandu and Pokhara offer outstanding infrastructure, experienced Indian faculties, and clinical training matching Indian patterns perfectly, with the massive advantage of zero passport or visa restrictions.

Program Quick Overview

Parameter Details
Degree Awarded MBBS (equivalent to Indian MBBS)
Course Duration 5.5 Years (including integrated clinical rotations)
Medium of Instruction 100% English (Clinical interactions in Nepali/Hindi)
Clinical Affiliation High patient-load Teaching Hospital Complexes
Recognitions WHO, NMC (India), Nepal Medical Council, and global boards

Eligibility Criteria & Admissions Checklist

Nepal medical admissions are highly structured and managed by the Medical Education Commission (MEC):

  1. Academic Performance: Minimum 50% aggregate score in 12th Standard Board Exams in PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology).
  2. MEC Entrance Exam: Students can secure admission either by passing the MEC entrance exam or registering directly using a valid NEET scorecard.
  3. NEET-UG: Qualified scorecard mandatory for registration and subsequent Indian licensing exams.

⏳ Admissions Note: Nepal colleges are extremely popular because of high clinical excellence. Admissions seats under the SAARC quota fill up very fast. Contact our team to plan your registration and verification.
